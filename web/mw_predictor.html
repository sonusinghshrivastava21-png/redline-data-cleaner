Model

  • HP: MS expands to CRH pressure at HP efficiency with full MS flow.
  • IP/LP: HRH expands to condenser pressure at IP/LP efficiency with MS flow × reheat fraction × IP/LP flow factor.
  • MW = (HP + IP/LP work) × mechanical × generator efficiency.
  • MS expands straight to condenser pressure at turbine efficiency with full MS flow.
  • MW = flow × enthalpy drop × mechanical × generator efficiency.
  • Feed-heating extractions are not modelled; calibrate to a measured MW to absorb them.
